1. The Hook & The Problem 0:00 – 0:45 (45s)
  • Scale: Billions in public/private tenders are published annually across PPIP and regional boards.
  • Friction: Fragmented portals require daily manual checks; rigid deadlines close without warning.
  • Bottleneck: Preparing compliance documentation and proposal skeletons takes 2–3 days per bid.
Script: "Every week, thousands of high-value tenders are posted across disjointed portals. For growing businesses, finding them in time and drafting compliance bids takes days of tedious paperwork. Most SMEs simply lack the staff to keep up."
2. The Solution — Quick Tenders 0:45 – 1:45 (60s)
  • Core Concept: Autonomous procurement agent for discovery, precision matching, and bid generation.
  • Key Differentiator: Not just an alert newsletter—it produces full, customized submission documents.
  • Efficiency Gain: Shrinks tender preparation time from 16+ hours to 15 minutes of review.
Script: "Quick Tenders solves this end-to-end. Our AI agent continuously monitors procurement feeds, matches opportunities against your company profile, and drafts the actual submission documents. You just review, sign, and send."
3. Product Walkthrough & Workflow 1:45 – 3:15 (90s)
  • Intelligent Scoring: Evaluates sector, industry, county proximity, and scale with clear fit scores (e.g. 85/100).
  • Zero-Touch DOCX Drafting: Generates editable Word files (Cover Letter & Technical Proposal Skeleton).
  • Multi-Channel Alerts: Real-time SMS (TextSMS) & Email (Resend) notifications with instant signed download links.
  • Pipeline Tracking: Built-in CRM states: New $\rightarrow$ Reviewed $\rightarrow$ Submitted with deadline urgency badges.
Script: "Here's the workflow in action: When a relevant tender appears, Quick Tenders scores the match and drafts a custom Cover Letter and Technical Proposal. The user downloads the editable Word file, adds their pricing, and marks it submitted. One rep can now run a 20-tender pipeline without any admin overhead."
4. Technical Architecture & Engineering Highlights 3:15 – 4:15 (60s)
  • Stack: Next.js 14 App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS.
  • Multi-Tenant Isolation: Supabase PostgreSQL with strict Row Level Security (RLS).
  • Dual-LLM Engine: Groq (Llama 3.3 70B) for high-speed inference with automatic fallback to xAI Grok.
  • Zero-Dependency DOCX Writer: Native streaming XML/ZIP generator via Node zlib—no bulky dependencies.
Script: "We built Quick Tenders for speed, security, and enterprise reliability. Company data is protected via Supabase RLS. We use a dual-LLM setup with Groq and xAI Grok for ultra-fast matching, and an in-house zero-dependency DOCX writer that generates proposals instantly."
5. Business Model & Market Fit 4:15 – 5:15 (60s)
  • Billing: Scalable subscription tiers integrated with Paystack for seamless regional payments.
  • Target Market: SME contractors, IT suppliers, logistics, medical distributors, and consulting firms.
  • Clear ROI: Winning a single additional contract or saving 40+ hours/month delivers immediate 10x ROI.
Script: "Our focus is on regional contractors and suppliers where procurement makes up a large share of business revenue. We monetize through simple subscription tiers via Paystack. The return on investment is immediate—saving dozens of staff hours on every single tender."
6. Roadmap & Closing 5:15 – 6:00 (45s)
  • Roadmap: Direct e-procurement portal auto-submission, tender win-probability analytics, compliance document vault.
  • Mission: Leveling the playing field so any ambitious business can win public procurement contracts.
Script: "Our mission is to level the playing field so every business can compete and win tenders. Thank you, and I’m ready for your questions!"
💡 Anticipated Q&A Cheat Sheet Presenter Reference
Q: How are AI hallucinations prevented?
Prompts strictly constrain the model to explicit tender requirements and verified profile data, generating editable Word drafts that keep humans in the loop.
Q: Why editable DOCX instead of PDF?
Real tender submissions require customized item pricing, schedules, and authorized signatures. Word documents provide an actionable working draft.
